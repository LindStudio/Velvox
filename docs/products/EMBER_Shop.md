## English

### EMBER De-Esser

**Tame the sibilance, keep the voice – one click on AUTO and Ember does the rest.**

- **AUTO in seconds:** Play your vocal, click Ember – he listens and sets frequency, threshold and range to match. Subtle by design, vowels stay untouched.
- **Smart detection:** Reacts only to real sibilants. Bright vowels, air and breaths stay as they are.
- **SIMPLE or ADVANCED:** One Amount knob and three voice types for the fast route – or full control with a spectrum, a draggable S range and threshold line.
- **Split or Wideband:** Turn down only the highs (dynamic high shelf) or dip the whole voice briefly – ideal for speech and podcasts.
- **DELTA listening:** Hear only what is removed and make confident decisions.
- **9 factory presets and A/B compare:** From Gentle to Strong, from Rap to Speech / podcast – plus your own presets.
- **Fixed 5 ms latency:** Reported to the host, unchanged in bypass.

### Meet Ember

Ember is a small red devil imp who lives in the middle of the plugin. With no signal he sits and smokes, while you sing he dances, and when sibilants hit he spits fire. Push the reduction too far and he overheats, coughing "Sounds lispy!" – so you see what is happening to your voice at a glance, without staring at numbers.

### System requirements

- Windows 10/11, 64-bit
- VST3 host (FL Studio, Ableton Live, Cubase, Reaper, Studio One, Bitwig …) or standalone app
- SSE2 CPU, 50 MB disk space
- Mac: not yet available
