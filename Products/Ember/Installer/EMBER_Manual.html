EMBER is a de-esser for vocals and speech from the VOCALYPSE family by Lind Audio. It turns down harsh S, Z and Sh sounds without touching the rest of the voice. Latency is always 5 ms; it is fixed and reported to your host so the host can compensate for it automatically.

1. Quick start

  1. Insert: Load EMBER as an insert on your vocal or speech track.
  2. AUTO: Play the vocal and click AUTO (or click Ember). Ember listens and sets frequency, threshold and range for you.
  3. Check: Press DELTA to hear only what is removed – that should be sibilants only. Adjust Amount if needed.

4. AUTO

Play the vocal and click AUTO or click Ember.

  • Ember listens to about 12 seconds of singing (pauses are skipped, up to 30 seconds), holding his hand to his ear. DONE ends listening early, from 3 seconds on.
  • EMBER then tries settings by rendering the take:
    • The frequency is set just below the strongest S (between 5 and 9.5 kHz).
    • Threshold and range are set so that S sounds lose 1.5 to 3.5 dB, depending on how harsh they are.
    • Vowels stay untouched (0.1 dB or less).
    • Release is set to 35 ms.
  • When AUTO is done, Ember gives a thumbs up.

The result is subtle by design. If you want more, raise Amount.

If EMBER finds too few S sounds, a message asks you to play a part with many S, Z or Sh sounds. Then start AUTO again.

5. Smart detection explained simply

A classic de-esser reacts to anything that is loud in the high frequencies. That includes bright vowels, air in the voice and breaths – the voice then sounds dull or lispy.

Smart detection therefore compares the S band with the body of the voice (250 Hz to 3 kHz). Only when the S band clearly stands out relative to the body is it a real sibilant, and EMBER steps in. Bright vowels, air and breaths stay untouched.

You can see this in the S LEVEL meter: when the bar is grey, Smart detection hears no sibilant.

8. Tips for great results

  • Start with AUTO and work from there.
  • 3 to 6 dB of range sounds natural. More can make the voice lisp.
  • Use DELTA: you should hear only sibilants. If you hear vowels or parts of words, lower Range or Amount, or raise the frequency.
  • For speech, try Wideband mode.
  • Put EMBER after EQ or compression that boosts the highs, and before reverb and delay.

10. Installation and uninstall

The installer places the files here:

ComponentLocation
VST3 pluginC:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\EMBER De-Esser.vst3
Standalone appC:\Program Files\Lind Audio\EMBER
ManualsC:\ProgramData\Lind Audio\EMBER

If EMBER does not appear right away, let your host rescan its plugins.

Uninstall: Windows Settings > Apps, select EMBER De-Esser and uninstall.

11. System requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • VST3 host (e.g. FL Studio, Ableton Live, Cubase, Reaper, Studio One, Bitwig …) or the included standalone app
  • CPU with SSE2
  • 50 MB free disk space

A Mac version is not yet available.
